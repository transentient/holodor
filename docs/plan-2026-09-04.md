# Plan of attack — Cliff's 8 points (written 2026-09-04, after the deep-suspend wedge)

Cliff's requirements, verbatim (2026-09-04):
1. Press = suspend with the lowest possible drain, AND if connected to power it charges normally.
2. Press to wake = it wakes, no controller / wifi / other hiccups.
3. Battery gauge accurate.
4. Battery drain normal and governed by the Decky settings.
5. Game performance optimal — crib from upstream, fix the rest ourselves.
6. Install from SD, install internal, boot back into Android, revert to Android: all safe and simple.
7. Headphone jack.
8. Everything else normal.

Rules for this run: **s2idle only, deep is banned** (plugged deep = hang, proven 09-04).
One variable per test. Nothing overnight that can wedge. Every fix ships as a package
bump + respin (repo/RC parity), never as a live-only edit.

Legend: [A] = agent work, no hands needed. [C] = ceremony, Cliff's hands. Status as of 09-04.

---

## Point 1 — suspend: lowest drain + charges normally when plugged

**Status.** Drain: DONE and shipped — s2idle measures 31 mA / 0.4 %/h overnight (08-30),
5–7× better than the old deep config. Charging: BROKEN — a plugged s2idle sleep charges for
~25–30 min then the charger firmware pauses (09-04 series: 20/40/160-min sleeps all gain
≈450–600 mAh; battery current ≈0 at the wake instant; PD contract stays up).

**Steps.**
- [A] **Stopgap: plugged-in self-wake.** Sleep `pre` hook: if on AC, arm an RTC alarm +25 min.
  `post` hook: if the wake was the RTC alarm, on AC, and no user input → keep the panel dark
  (skip powerd's brightness restore), re-suspend after ~20 s via `systemd-run --collect
  --on-active`. Each wake restarts the firmware's charge window (proven by the series).
  Depends on the grace-race fix (Point 2a) so the re-suspend path is deterministic.
  Done when: plugged overnight from ≤60 % reaches the 100/95 threshold, telemetry shows it.
- [A] **Real fix: find what the firmware keys on.** Three candidates, tested one at a time
  with a plugged 60-min s2idle slice (`sleepcharge-ab.sh 60`; pause ⇒ ≈500 mAh, no pause ⇒
  ≥1000 mAh): (i) dwc3 `power/control=on` / hold the USB link out of suspend; (ii) force
  USB_ROLE_NONE for charger-only PD partners even when they advertise USB data (0519's
  intent — this charger dodges it; ALSO the likely root of the plugged-deep hang, so it is
  worth having regardless); (iii) unbind/rebind dwc3 around suspend. Winner becomes a
  kernel patch or a sleep hook; then the stopgap can be retired or kept as belt-and-braces.
- [A] **Never brown out again:** upower `PercentageAction=5`, `CriticalPowerAction=PowerOff`;
  powerd refuses suspend <5 % (shuts down) and logs inhibitor refusals. Ships in bsp-common.
- Optional [A]: knee test `sleepcharge-ab.sh 5 10 15 20 30 45` (s2idle only) to time the pause.

## Point 2 — wake with no hiccups

**Status.** Three distinct failures, all now named:
- (a) **Two presses to wake / instant re-suspend — ROOT-CAUSED 09-04.** The post-resume grace
  inhibitor races logind ("operation … is already running"), fails ~1 in 6 wakes, and
  Steam's idle clock (which counted the sleep) re-suspends 1 s later. Fallout on 09-04:
  Steam "another account is playing", game orphaned from the UI.
- (b) **Wifi sulk after wake** — roam → WCN7860 firmware crash; wifi-heal never ran (cgroup
  teardown). Research in docs/wifi-roam-crash-research.md.
- (c) **Dead controls after resume (in-game)** — open. Same Steam-Input activation-race family
  as the Vampire Survivors launch bug (controller.txt flapping). Not yet instrumented.

**Steps.**
- [A] (a) `wake-grace-post`: retry `systemd-inhibit` until logind accepts (≤20 × 0.1 s), or
  move the grace into powerd on logind's PrepareForSleep(false). bsp-common bump. Also commit
  the live powerd prototype (dim-panel restore, grace-cancel) — it is running uncommitted.
- [A] (b) iwd `DisableRoamingScan=true` + 5 GHz rank bias; wifi-heal rewritten to
  `nmcli connection up <profile>` on resume and on the kernel "pdev 0 successfully recovered"
  event, launched with `systemd-run --collect`; consider kernel-25 = ROCKNIX PR 3189 + 3171.
- [A]+[C] (c) Instrument: log evdev device add/remove and Steam's controller.txt around a
  suspend-mid-game; Cliff reproduces twice. Then fix (keep the gamepad node stable across
  suspend, or nudge Steam Input post-resume).
- [C] **Validation ceremony (the release gate):** 20 wake cycles, half mid-game, half at
  Steam home, some plugged: one press each, controls live within 3 s, wifi back within 10 s,
  no Steam dialogs. Scoreboard in HANDOFF.

## Point 3 — accurate battery gauge

**Status.** The gauge is the charger firmware's (battmgr); we can't calibrate it, only stop
abusing it. It was wrecked by the 09-02 brownout death and is relearning (upowerd "nan",
33 %→10 % in 17 min, charge_counter rewrites).

**Steps.**
- [C] **Relearn cycle now:** leave it plugged and awake-or-self-waking to 100 % (thresholds
  are 100/95), then one normal play discharge to <20 %, then charge to 100 % again.
- [A] Brownout prevention (Point 1) so it never happens again.
- [A] Telemetry watch for a week (pocknix-telemetry is on): % vs charge_counter vs voltage.
  If drift persists after the relearn it is firmware behaviour → document, and show the
  Decky battery panel with a time-to-empty derived from current_now rather than %.
- Done when: a full discharge shows % falling monotonically ≈ coulomb count, no jumps >3 %.

## Point 4 — drain normal, governed by Decky

**Status.** Sleep drain fine. Awake anomalies unexplained (785 mA hot nap 09-01, 2.85 A awake
hour) — no data existed; telemetry now catches the next one. `max` power mode persisted across
reboots once (08-17) and burned a day.

**Steps.**
- [A] `max` non-persistent (revert to balanced on boot); benches must restore modes.
- [A] Decky power panel = the single source of truth: mode, fan, idle-suspend timeout, and the
  new AC-aware policy (no idle-suspend while plugged is a candidate — Cliff's call).
- [A] Steam download suspend-inhibitor unit (SteamOS has one, we don't).
- [C] 3-day normal-use telemetry review; any hour >1.5 A awake-idle gets a root cause.
- Done when: 3 days of telemetry with no unexplained draw, and the mode chosen in Decky is the
  mode applied after a reboot.

## Point 7 — headphone jack

**Status.** Audio through the jack works; auto-detection of a plug (MBHC) does not. Vendor
DTB ground truth already mined. The open question is whether stock Android auto-detects.

**Steps.**
- [C] **THE JACK TEST on stock Android** during the Phase-2 block, first thing after restore.
- Android detects → [A] resume the MBHC detect hunt (vendor archaeology, wcd codec bindings).
- Android doesn't → manual output switching is platform parity → [A] make the switch a
  one-tap Decky control and document it. Close the hunt.
- Done when: either auto-switch works, or the one-tap switch ships with the doc line.

## Point 8 — everything else normal

rename; Decky self-update guard (we ship it pinned).

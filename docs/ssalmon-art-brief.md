# HOLODOR: new art request

Three new pieces, in the same style as the boot cutscene you made, using the same palette you used last time (DawnBringer 32).

Some background: Holodor now has its own bootloader. It's the first thing that runs when the handheld is switched on, before your cutscene. It shows a picture while it starts up, and it has a menu for choosing between Android and Holodor. These pieces are for that screen, and for a small Android app that restarts the handheld into Holodor.

## 1. App icon: Holodor's head

- The icon for the "Boot Holodor" Android app. We'll also use it in docs and on the website.
- A close-up of Holodor's head, matching your character design. He should look friendly and happy.
- Canvas: 64×64, the same size as the full-body Holodor you made last time. 32×32 is fine too if it reads better.
- Keep the head inside the middle two-thirds of the square. Android crops app icons into circles and rounded squares.
- Transparent background.

## 2. Boot picture: Holodor running from zombies / white walkers

- Shown full screen at power-on, before the cutscene. The idea is that this is act 1 (Holodor running for his life) and the cutscene is act 2 (holding the door).
- Holodor should be wearing his backpack. The hidden Steam orb in it doesn't need to show if the picture looks better from another angle.
- Canvas: 240×135, the same grid as the cutscene. We scale it 8× to fill the 1920×1080 screen.
- Black background (#000000).
- Two versions if the wordmark fits the picture: one without text, and one with the new HOLODOR wordmark (piece 3).

## 3. A new HOLODOR wordmark

- A variation on your current HOLODOR logo: a little different, not a radical change. It's for the top of the bootloader menu and for the boot picture.
- About the same size as your current logo (156×32), and no more than 32 px tall. The menu will show it at 3× in a header bar 108 px tall.
- It has to read on black: the menu background is pure black.
- Transparent background.

## Files

- PNGs at their native size, plus the .aseprite files, like last time. We'll do all the scaling.

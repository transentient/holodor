## Device

AYN Odin 3 (Snapdragon 8 Elite). Boots from SD next to Android; an internal install is optional.

## What changed since 20260927d

- Headphone jack works out of the box. The 20260927d image left it silent until the first update.
- Headphones are balanced. The left channel was 2 to 3 dB quieter than the right. The jack now uses the same audio path as stock Android, and left and right measure within 0.1 dB at every volume step.
- Kernel 7.1.3-33 and device support 27 carry the fix. They are installed together.
- The bootloader restore tool recognises ROCKNIX ABL 1.2.

## Already on Holodor?

Update from Settings (or `pacman -Syu` in desktop mode). No reflash needed.

## Known issues

- Unreal Engine 5 games launch but are not yet playable.
